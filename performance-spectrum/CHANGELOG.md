# Changelog

## 0.1.9

Changelog starts here; versions before 0.1.9 were not individually tracked.

Fine-grained performance analysis after Denisov, Fahland, and van der
Aalst: every observed directly-follows segment is plotted over calendar
time and coloured by its duration class, exposing batching, queuing,
slowdowns, and drift hidden by aggregated averages. On OCELs it can
consume source-bound Interaction Atlas lasso cohorts.
